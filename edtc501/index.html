About EDTC 501

Graduate-level course exploring research-based online pedagogy with focus on Community of Inquiry framework, cognitive load theory, backward design, constructive alignment, and evidence-based assessment. Designed for practitioner-scholars in higher education and K-12 contexts.

For Instructors: These materials are freely available for adaptation and use. If you're teaching similar content, feel free to link to these resources or adapt them for your context. Attribution appreciated but optional per CC-BY license terms.
